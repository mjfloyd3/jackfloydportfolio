Template Systems for Product and Marketing Communications

Highlighting market expertise for improved product communication & impact

Company
BlueMatrix
Role
Lead Designer & Developer
Tools

What Communications Looked Like Before

Client-facing communications and release notes were built manually in Word and sent to customers as static PDF attachments.

I partnered with our Chief Client Officer, product managers, and marketing team to replace that fragmented workflow with a structured template system built directly inside the same publishing platform our clients use every day.

The goals were to:

  • Eliminate manual formatting: Move authors out of Word and static PDFs into a structured CMS workflow.
  • Publish once, distribute everywhere: Generate PDF, web, and email versions from a single source so customers can easily share updates downstream.
  • Establish brand trust: Give all official communications a unified, professional look.

Inside the Template System

Standardized Content Structure

Product release notes were our starting point. Every note follows a consistent architecture: an executive summary (which automatically populates the email introduction) followed by modular body components. To keep longer updates easy to scan, a configurable table of contents sits alongside the main content and adapts seamlessly to smaller screens.

Streamlined Authoring Workflow

Instead of wrestling with layout and spacing, authors shape a note using lightweight metadata tags:

  • Product Logo Tag: Automatically places the appropriate product branding at the top of the note.
  • Feature Highlight Tags: Applied via a component's attributes menu (Announcement, New Feature, Improvement, Tip, Security, and Deprecated) to surface key updates at a glance. Components can carry multiple tags as needed.
  • Smart Attachments: When a release calendar is attached, a dedicated action button automatically appears beside View PDF for quick access.

Typography, including headings, image captions, sources, and footnotes, is governed by strict system styles, ensuring every note stays on-brand without manual intervention.

From Web Page to Inbox

Each note publishes simultaneously to web, PDF, and email from a single source of truth. The email version pulls the note’s summary as its opening hook and includes a direct CTA to the full web version, allowing authors to write once while keeping every channel in sync.

Practicing What We Preach: Scaling Internal Comms

What previously required hours of manual formatting now happens automatically, keeping every client communication on-brand by default.

This consistency strengthened customer trust and gave internal teams a repeatable framework, eliminating the need to reinvent layouts for every send. Just as importantly, it served as real-world proof of our CMS's capabilities, allowing us to run our own external communications on the exact product our clients rely on.
