What Communications Looked Like Before

Client-facing communications, release notes, and other materials were regularly built in Word, where content authors sent out PDF attachments to communicate product updates.

Instead of relying on these outdated methods, I worked with our Chief Client Officer, product managers, and marketing team to build a template system inside of our own CMS (the same one our clients use) that let authors write directly into a structured, on-brand format, publishing consistent communications across every channel from a single source.

Beyond an updated look and feel, the primary goals were to:

  • move authors off Word docs and PDF attachments into our own CMS, using the same platform we build for clients
  • provide multi-formatted versions (PDF, web and email) to customers for simplified sharing downstream
  • publish from a single source across every channel instead of maintaining separate versions per format
  • bring a unified visual identity and format to our official communications to establish trust, reliability and professionalism among our customers
