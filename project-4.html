What Communications Looked Like Before

Client-facing communications and release notes were built in Word and sent to customers as PDF attachments.

I worked with our Chief Client Officer, product managers, and marketing team to replace that with a template system inside our own CMS, the same one our clients use. Authors write directly into a structured, on-brand format and publish to every channel from a single source.

The goals were to:

  • move authors off Word and PDF attachments and into the CMS
  • deliver PDF, web and email versions from one source, so customers can share them downstream
  • give official communications a unified look that builds trust and professionalism

Practicing What We Preach: Scaling Internal Comms

What used to require hours of manual formatting now happens automatically, ensuring every communication stays on-brand by default.
